# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project designed to analyze and monitor the overall performance of an e-commerce business.
This dashboard provides deep insights into sales, profit, quantity sold, average order value (AOV), customer behavior, product categories, payment methods, and monthly profit trends.

The goal of this project is to help businesses and stakeholders make data-driven decisions by identifying profitable products, understanding customer purchasing patterns, and tracking regional and temporal sales performance.

# 🎯 Objectives

Analyze overall sales and profit performance

Track monthly profit trends to identify peak and loss periods

Understand customer contribution to revenue

Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories

Analyze payment mode preferences

Enable region-wise filtering for better decision-making

# 🧩 Key Metrics (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights the following key performance indicators:

Total Sales Amount – Overall revenue generated

Total Quantity Sold – Number of products sold

Total Profit – Net profit across all transactions

Average Order Value (AOV) – Average value per order

These KPIs give a quick snapshot of the business health at a glance.

# 📊 Dashboard Features & Visualizations

# 🔹 Sales by State

Displays total sales amount by state

Helps identify high-performing and low-performing regions

Supports region-based business strategy

# 🔹 Monthly Profit Analysis

Bar chart showing profit trends across months

Identifies profit dips and loss-making months

Useful for seasonality and trend analysis

# 🔹 Category-wise Quantity Distribution

Donut chart representing quantity sold by category

Categories include:

Clothing

Furniture

Electronics

Helps understand customer demand patterns

# 🔹 Sub-Category Profit Analysis

Horizontal bar chart showing profit by sub-category

Highlights top and low-profit products such as:

Printers

Phones

Sarees

Tables

Useful for inventory and pricing decisions

# 🔹 Customer-wise Sales Contribution

Bar chart showing sales amount by customer

Identifies high-value customers

Helps in customer segmentation and retention strategies

# 🔹 Payment Mode Analysis

Donut chart showing quantity by payment mode

Includes:

COD

UPI

Credit Card

EMI

Helps understand customer payment preferences

# 🎛 Interactive Filters & Controls

Quarter-wise filter (Q1–Q4) for time-based analysis

State-wise filter for regional insights

Interactive visuals allow users to drill down and explore data dynamically

# 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation

Microsoft Excel / CSV – Data source and preprocessing

Data Cleaning & Transformation – Power Query

DAX – KPI calculations and measures

# 📈 Business Insights

Clothing category contributes the highest quantity of sales

Certain months show negative profit, indicating cost or pricing issues

COD and UPI are the most preferred payment modes

A small group of customers contributes a significant portion of revenue

Some sub-categories generate high sales but low profit, highlighting optimization opportunities
